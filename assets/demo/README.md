- 首页三张截图分别展示分区点阵、屏边点阵和4×4静态点阵。原图为2559×1599，转换为高质量JPEG并移除元数据，未裁切或改变点阵排布与颜色。

Captured on 2026-11-08, showing MotionCompensator 0.1.0-preview.1 running over PAYDAY 2.

- The three homepage screenshots show Regional, Screen-edge and 4x4 Static dots. Originals are 2559x1599, converted to high-quality JPEG with metadata removed. Dot layouts and colors were not altered, and images were not cropped.
- The Regional GIF is 800x500 at 25 fps for approximately three seconds. It preserves playback speed without frame interpolation. Source capture targeted 60 fps and averaged approximately 48.25 fps.
- Two supplementary 1280x800 stills come from an earlier 12 fps recording.
- Dots were rendered by the app. Processing is limited to excerpt selection, scaling, sampling and compression. The media illustrates appearance and motion, not processing latency, compatibility or comfort efficacy.
